Why "Spoon Theory"?

Spoon theory was written by Christine Miserandino to describe living with limited energy. Each task costs some spoons, and when they are gone, they are gone for the day.

The app borrows that idea. Focus is a resource to spend with care, and resting is part of the plan, not a failure of it.
