# Privacy Policy

**Spoon Theory**
Effective date: October 20, 2026

Spoon Theory is a focus timer for iPhone, Mac, and Apple Watch, published by Alex Plischke.

## Data Collection

Spoon Theory doesn't collect any data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and nothing is shared or sold.

## iCloud Sync

The app syncs your presets and history between your devices using your private iCloud account. I can't access that data, and since I don't hold any of it, there's nothing on my end to delete.

## Purchases

The app is a one-time purchase through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and I don't receive your personal information.

## Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the effective date above.
